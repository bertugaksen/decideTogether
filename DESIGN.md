# Design System: DecideTogether (Warm Editorial Minimal)

## Core Philosophy
- Aesthetic: Anti-futuristic, warm editorial, flat minimalist, analog feel.
- Zero AI-slop: NO neon glows, NO dark cyber aesthetics, NO complex gradients, NO bottom tab bars.
- Psychology: Appetite-inducing warm tones (gastronomy-focused), low cognitive load.

## Color Palette
- Background: #FBF9F5 (Warm Cream / Linen Paper)
- Surface Card: #FFFFFF (Crisp White Card with 1px border #E7E2D9)
- Primary Accent: #D9381E (Paprika Red - stimulates appetite and quick decision)
- Secondary Accent: #E07A5F (Warm Terracotta / Baked Clay)
- Match / Action: #2D6A4F (Basil Green - fresh, positive resolution)
- Text Primary: #1C1917 (Deep Charcoal / Warm Black)
- Text Secondary: #78716C (Muted Warm Stone)

## Typography & Elements
- Font: Clean, elegant modern grotesque (Inter or Neue Haas feel).
- Cards: Minimalist physical polaroid/magazine layout. Generous whitespace around high-quality food photography.
- Buttons: Solid flat color, no gradients, no inner glows. Tactile 48px height.
- Layout: Pure linear flow. Absolutely NO bottom navigation bar.
